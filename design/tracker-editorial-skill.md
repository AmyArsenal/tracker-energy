# tracker.energy Editorial Standard

Voice: a senior regulatory analyst who respects the reader's time. WaPo clarity, RTO Insider depth, zero hype.

## Structure
- Lead with the verdict: what happened and why it matters, in the first two sentences. No throat-clearing.
- Then the evidence: what the filing says, cited to exact passages.
- Then the context: where this sits in the docket/lifecycle (stakeholder discussion -> filing -> tariff change).
- Then honest limits: what we don't know, what's contested, what to watch next.

## Rules
- Every claim traces to a cited passage. Allegation vs finding vs holding is never blurred: "X argues" is not "FERC held".
- Companies, dockets, and people are named precisely (accession numbers, docket IDs).
- Numbers are exact and sourced; no "significant", "major", "many" without a figure.
- Plain words: "filed" not "submitted for consideration", "blocked" not "potentially impeded".
- Procedural filings get one-line treatment unless they change the timeline.
- No filler, no "in conclusion", no restating the headline as an ending.
- Related reads point sideways (same docket, same company, same issue), not just "more news".

## Formatting
- Full-page editorial layout (Halcyon-grade restraint: whitespace, one accent, editorial type).
- Inline citation links on the claims themselves; PDF opens split-screen at the passage.
- Summary box up top for skimmers; depth below for analysts.

## Two registers (owner-set 2026-09-12)

ANALYST REGISTER - docket summaries, filing detail, query answers, technical sections:
- Technical analyst level, written for lawyers and consultants. No jokes, no catchy framing, no dumbing down. Precision over accessibility.

NEWS REGISTER - News/Latest section, X, LinkedIn:
- Explanatory and accessible: catchy headline, hero image, plain-English "what happened and why it matters" that a smart outsider can follow. Still sharp-analyst credibility: no memes, no bullshit jokes, no hype adjectives. A lawyer reading it should nod, not wince.
- Selection gate: only stories that pass the so-what test go social. Not every filing is a post.

Same evidence and citations underneath both. News register translates; it never distorts.
