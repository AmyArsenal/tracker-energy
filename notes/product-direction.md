# Product direction (Sep 10, 2026) — from tracker site to one workflow

## The moment
On June 18, 2026 FERC issued Section 206 show-cause orders directing ALL SIX
RTOs/ISOs to justify or rewrite their large-load interconnection tariffs
(Docket RM26-4 + per-RTO proceedings). Every rule that decides when and how
data centers get power is being rewritten simultaneously, across FERC, six
ISOs, and state commissions. Comment deadlines, compliance filings, and
committee votes are landing weekly across ~15 venues at once.

## Sharp user
Regulatory affairs / interconnection lead at a data-center developer, IPP, or
hyperscaler energy team — and the law-firm associates who serve them.

## Urgent pain
Missing one comment deadline, compliance filing, or committee vote in one of
the simultaneous proceedings moves project timelines by quarters. Today they
track this with FERC eSubscription firehose emails, law-firm alert PDFs, and
spreadsheets. No one consolidates the record across venues with analysis.

## The one workflow (daily, repeatable)
"What changed overnight in the proceedings I follow, what does it mean, and
what do I need to do?" Open the Watch -> ruled digest of the last 24h/7d ->
click one filing -> staff analysis beside the annotated source PDF -> follow
the docket -> tomorrow's digest is personalized.

## Wedge
The free, public, nightly "large-load interconnection record" — FERC + RTO
show-cause dockets + PJM committees + state commissions, one screen, primary
sources linked, staff analysis on the hot filings. Accuracy and citation
(annotation spans into the source PDF) over volume.

## Competition / why us
- FERC eSubscription: raw firehose, no cross-venue, no analysis.
- Halcyon ($21M Series A, Energize): broad energy-reg intelligence platform,
  seat-licensed; we win on interconnection depth + free public record.
- FiscalNote/Quorum: broad policy tracking, legislation-first, no
  document-level energy depth.
- Law firm alerts (McGuireWoods, Bracewell): episodic, marketing-shaped.
- RTO Insider: news coverage, not a monitoring workflow.

## Compounding data advantage
Canonical normalized corpus (580+ docs, +nightly) across FERC/PUC/ISO/PJM
with deterministic taxonomy tags, per-filing staff analysis, and PDF-region
annotations. Backfile deepens daily; tagging rules improve with curation;
nobody else has this cross-venue normalized record.

## Retention loop
Watchlist/follow -> nightly digest -> deadline and vote reminders -> weekly
per-proceeding summary. First tab with morning coffee.

## 30-second demo
Land: "LARGE LOAD INTERCONNECTION — what changed in the last 24h across
FERC, six RTOs, and state commissions." Ruled list. Click top item. Split
view: analysis + annotated PDF. Follow. Understood in 30s.

## What changes on the site
- Landing page becomes THE WATCH (the workflow above). Battery fleet map
  demotes to a Data section (still maintained nightly, still linked).
- FERC newsroom reframed as the federal stream of the Watch.
- ISO/PUC pages stay as the venue views of the same record.
- Search stays as the discovery layer (580-doc corpus).
- No new breadth until the Watch is exceptional.

## Design system (from the reference research)
"Register" language: warm paper, ink, ONE signal-orange accent (#c8501a);
Archivo (wide grotesque) for UI/display, Newsreader for longform headlines,
IBM Plex Mono for data/labels; ruled lists not cards; bracketed mono tags
not pills; no stock photos, no gradients, no dark marketing hero, no glow.
References: RTO Insider (density, ruled lists, section color coding),
Claude (restraint, one action), Halcyon app (summary-first result rows,
left filter rail), Legora (typographic confidence), WaPo (editorial trust).
